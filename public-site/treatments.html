Complete Dental Care

Our Dental Treatments

Discover our range of dental care services at Sakthi Dental Clinic, Hosur. From routine cleaning to restorative and cosmetic treatments, our team can help you understand the options suitable for your needs.

Treatment 01

Teeth Cleaning

Professional teeth cleaning helps remove plaque and tartar buildup from the teeth. Regular dental cleaning can support healthier gums and better oral hygiene.

Your dentist can recommend a cleaning schedule based on your oral health.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 02

Tooth Filling

Dental fillings repair teeth affected by cavities or minor damage. The dentist removes the decayed portion and fills the area with a suitable dental material.

Early evaluation may help prevent further tooth damage.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 03

Teeth Extraction

Tooth extraction involves removing a tooth when it cannot be adequately preserved or when removal is recommended for oral health reasons.

The dentist will explain the procedure, aftercare and possible replacement options where appropriate.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 04

Complete Dentures

Complete dentures are removable dental appliances designed to replace all teeth in an upper or lower jaw. They can help restore appearance, speech and everyday chewing function.

Fit and comfort depend on individual needs and the shape of the gums and jaw.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 05

Dental Implants

Dental implants are placed in the jawbone to support replacement teeth. They may be considered for replacing missing teeth and restoring chewing function.

Suitability depends on bone health, general health and a professional dental assessment.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 06

Laser Dentistry

Laser dentistry uses focused light energy for selected dental and soft-tissue procedures. Whether it is appropriate depends on the condition being treated and the dentist's assessment.

Ask your dentist about the benefits, limitations and alternatives for your specific procedure.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 07

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment addresses infection or damage inside a tooth. The affected tissue is treated, and the tooth is cleaned and sealed to help preserve it.

The final restoration depends on the condition and structure of the treated tooth.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 08

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth may need removal when they are impacted, cause repeated problems or affect nearby teeth. An examination and dental X-rays may be required before treatment.

Not every wisdom tooth needs to be removed.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 09

Dental Bridge

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by supporting an artificial tooth between neighbouring teeth or other suitable dental supports.

Your dentist can explain whether a bridge, implant or another replacement option is suitable for you.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 10

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening can help reduce certain types of tooth discolouration. The results vary depending on the cause of staining and the natural colour of the teeth.

A dental check-up is recommended before whitening treatment.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 11

Dental Veneers

Veneers are thin coverings bonded to the front surface of selected teeth. They may be used to improve the appearance of certain stains, chips, gaps or shape differences.

Your dentist will discuss the expected results and any preparation that may be needed.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 12

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric dental care focuses on children's oral health, including check-ups, preventive guidance and assessment of developing teeth and gums.

Regular visits can help children develop healthy oral hygiene habits from an early age.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 13

Flap Surgery

Periodontal flap surgery is a gum procedure that may be recommended for selected cases of advanced gum disease. It allows the dentist to access areas around the teeth for treatment.

Treatment planning depends on gum health and the severity of the condition.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 14

Orthodontic Braces

Orthodontic braces gradually move teeth into a better position. They may help address crowding, spacing and some bite alignment concerns.

Treatment duration and the best appliance depend on an orthodontic assessment.

Book an Appointment
Treatment 15

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable transparent trays designed to gradually move teeth. They may be an option for selected alignment concerns following an orthodontic assessment.

Wearing schedules and results depend on the treatment plan and consistent use as directed.
