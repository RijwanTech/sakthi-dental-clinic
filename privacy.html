Privacy Policy

Information about how this website handles information you choose to share with Sakthi Dental Clinic.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Introduction

Sakthi Dental Clinic respects your privacy. This policy describes how information may be collected and used when you visit this website or contact the clinic using the contact details provided.

2. Information You May Provide

If you use the appointment enquiry form, you may be asked to provide your name, email address, phone number, treatment of interest, and an optional message. Please avoid including sensitive personal or medical information in the enquiry form.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry.
  • Contact you about an appointment request.
  • Provide information about the services you ask about.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the website.

4. Form Services and Data Transmission

The website may use a third-party form service or a clinic-managed server to transmit enquiries. If such a service is enabled, the provider may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. The website owner should identify the actual provider and update this section before the form is launched.

Until a form delivery service is configured, submitting the website form does not guarantee that the clinic receives your information.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use basic technical features required for its operation. If analytics tools, advertising technologies, or additional cookies are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them and explain any applicable choices available to visitors.

6. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website or respond to enquiries, or when disclosure is required by applicable law. The clinic should not claim that information is never shared unless its actual practices support that statement.

7. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information submitted through the website. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information should be retained only as long as needed for its stated purpose or as required by applicable law.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact the clinic to ask about information you have submitted, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the clinic's actual data-handling practices.

9. External Links

This website may link to external services, including map providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review before using them.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide general clinic information. Parents or guardians should provide children's personal information only when appropriate and in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website features or information handling practices change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
